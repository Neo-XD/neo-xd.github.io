Why Nocturne

Everything the web player should have been

Built from the ground up for high fidelity, instant performance, and distraction-free listening.

🚫

No ads, ever

Nocturne plays the audio stream directly, so there is nothing to interrupt. No video pre-rolls, no ad breaks, no subscription needed.

⚡

Light on your PC

A native Rust core and Tauri architecture instead of a bundled browser. Opens instantly, sips memory, and keeps your fans quiet.

🎧

Gapless, tuned sound

mpv-powered audio engine with true gapless playback, loudness normalization, and custom equalizer profiles.

🎤

Lyrics that follow along

Time-synced live lyrics scroll automatically with the song, line-by-line and syllable-by-syllable with karaoke timing.

👥

Listen Together

Host shared listening sessions, generate an invite code, and stream music in perfect real-time synchronization with friends.

📚

Your library, intact

Sign in once and your playlists, likes, artists, albums, and subscriptions are right there. Any changes sync back to YouTube Music.

⌨️ Media keys & shortcuts 📊 Last.fm scrobbling 🎮 Discord Rich Presence 📁 Multi-level playlist folders ✨ Fluid glass & ambient themes 🔄 Seamless auto-updates

01 / Live Lyrics

Sing every word

Synced lyrics stay locked to the rhythm. The active lyric line illuminates with dynamic ambient glow while previous verses smoothly fade back.

  • ✓ Word-by-word karaoke synchronization
  • ✓ Customizable lyrics typography and local fonts
  • ✓ Dual-source lyric fallback for maximum coverage
02 / Discography

Go down the rabbit hole

Explore artists, albums, singles, moods, and mixes. The entire YouTube Music catalog rendered in a crisp, ultra-fast native window with instant search.

  • ✓ Search anything with Ctrl+Space
  • ✓ High-resolution album artwork and artist bios
  • ✓ Clean tracklist hierarchy and related recommendations
03 / Social Playback

Press play with friends

Start a Listen Together session and share a quick invite code. Play, pause, seek, and queue adjustments stay synchronized for every participant.

  • ✓ Sub-second playback synchronization
  • ✓ Collaborative queue control with room permissions
  • ✓ Works across local Wi-Fi, Tailscale, or public rooms

Version History

Latest release notes

Highlights from recent Nocturne updates.

🖥️ Desktop

Nocturne Desktop

Nocturne Desktop v0.8.3 16 Sept 2026
Profile Spotify linking, playlist sync & BetterLyrics viewing tech
Moved Spotify authentication directly into user profile dropdown with a Spotify Premium notice banner. Added 3-way playlist handling modes (Separate, In Sync, Transfer) with dual sidebar tabs for YTM and Spotify playlists and 1-click track transfers. Replaced oversized info sidebar spacing with BetterLyrics viewing tech (dual-edge gradient alpha masking, smooth centering auto-scroll, and syllable glow). Added toggleable audio quality badge and decoupled Listen Together handoffs.
Nocturne Desktop v0.8.2 13 Sept 2026
Motion album art, 8 lyric styles & resizable sidebar
Looping video art integration from Nocturne Canvas and Apple Music catalog. 8 distinct selectable lyric animation styles. Fullscreen now playing edge-to-edge acrylic translucency. Resizable left navigation sidebar with interactive drag handle (200px–420px).
Nocturne Desktop v0.8.1 11 Sept 2026
Audio engine optimization & memory enhancements
Optimized mpv IPC audio stream processing, reduced webview memory consumption during heavy playlist browsing, and stability improvements across all OS platforms.
Nocturne Desktop v0.8.0 10 Sept 2026
Liquid Glass theme, floating player bar & audio crossfade
Acrylic translucent floating panels with backdrop blur, floating player bar with rounded corners, smooth right sidebar docking with dynamic width transitions, native mpv audio crossfade (0–12s), and Discord Rich Presence customization suite.
Nocturne Desktop v0.7-d 2 Sept 2026
Hardware acceleration, PIN auth & fullscreen polish
Added hardware acceleration support, re-added PIN authentication for remote control features, simplified Last.fm integration, true borderless fullscreen player, and UI polish across the app.
📱 Mobile

Nocturne Mobile

Nocturne Mobile v0.3 16 Sept 2026
Decoupled Listen Together, direct Spotify linking & typography restoration
Direct Spotify account linking with cookie authentication (psst-style). Decoupled Listen Together room sessions from audio output routing, allowing seamless device switching in-room. Restored high-contrast typography across all 10 themes, fixed corrupted <?> play count glyphs, merged profile & settings popover, and modern skeleton loaders.
Nocturne Mobile v0.2.4-m 11 Sept 2026
Syllable lyric alignment, queue reordering animations & performance
Syllable-level synchronized lyric highlighting, smooth drag-and-drop queue reordering animations, and enhanced battery efficiency.
Nocturne Mobile v0.2.3-m 8 Sept 2026
Remote control handoff polish, background sync & media controls
Sub-second remote playback synchronization with Nocturne Desktop, rich Android media notification controls, and auto-reconnect logic.
Nocturne Mobile v0.2.2-m 5 Sept 2026
Material 3 Expressive theme enhancements & Axion EQ
Material 3 Expressive dynamic color theming, Axion EQ equalizer presets, and enhanced library navigation hierarchy.
Nocturne Mobile v0.2-m 2 Sept 2026
Bug fixes, improved navigation & sync foundation
Added the base for improved desktop sync, cleaner navigation hierarchy, performance improvements, and codebase cleanups.
Nocturne Mobile v0.1 29 Aug 2026
The first Nocturne Music release for Android
Full remote-controller mode for desktop playback, standalone Android player with dynamic ambient backgrounds (Apple Music Fluid Blur, Live Mesh), and bidirectional handoff.
